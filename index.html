About Our Church

Rooted in Scripture, Reaching Our Community

Our History

Gofe Genut Evangelical Church was established through the grace of God and the dedication of faithful believers in the Sidama region. What began as a humble fellowship prayer group has blossomed into a vibrant beacon of light and spiritual revival.

Today, our congregation embraces families and youths from all walks of life, united in worshiping our Lord Jesus Christ and transforming our community through Christian love and biblical truth.

Biblical Foundation

All scripture is inspired by God and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness.

Our Vision

To see transformed lives across Sidama and beyond through the power of the Gospel, creating a spiritually mature and loving church community.

Our Mission

To worship God in spirit and truth, make disciples of Jesus Christ, equip believers for ministry, and extend compassionate service to the needy.

Core Values

Christ-centered living, biblical authority, fervent prayer, loving fellowship, integrity, and proactive community evangelism.
